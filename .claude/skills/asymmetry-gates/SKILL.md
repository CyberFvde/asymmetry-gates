# Asymmetry Gates

Policy: **Oct 24, 2026, revision 7** under #18 (route D for dilutive growth; revision 6 return-estimate fix, 20% screen guardrail, #14a 10% growth band and M route retained); FT supporting rules remain Oct 2 revision 2 apart from the route-name sync. The dated roster is a separate evidence snapshot.

A rules-based screen targeting 25%+ annualized returns in a concentrated, high-asymmetry growth sleeve while surviving the name you're wrong about. This is an objective, not a promised return or a new per-name eligibility hurdle. A–E preserves strict core quality; G evaluates proven compounders on per-share economics; H admits only capped, funded early transitions with a fixed quality destination; M admits starter-size positions in mega-cap platforms whose FCF is depressed by growth capex, with a fixed recovery clock; D admits starter-size positions in self-funding high-growth companies that issue stock, measuring dilution forward and requiring a 25% per-share return after it. Fintech receives stricter treatment. PH makes prolonged price underperformance affect capital eligibility even when fundamentals pass. Every position carries written, numeric kill triggers before it gets capital. Assess both the opportunity and the risk; passing a gate needs no additional invented hurdle, and an empty shortlist is acceptable when the evidence warrants it. A durable theme merits research, not an assumption of indefinite stock-price growth.

## How to run a screen

1. Load `framework.md` and `price-health.md`. Identify sector and stage before applying gates: FT overlay first for fintech; F for levered power/IPPs and commodity names; A–E for strict core; G for an evidenced, cash-generating compounder; H for a funded early transition; M for a ≥$500B platform failing A2/A3 because of growth capex; D for a ≥$10B self-funding high-growth company failing A1/G3 on equity or equity-linked issuance. Read the selected supporting file. Prefer A–E when it qualifies; never use G/H/M/D to evade FT/F/PH routing or an unidentified failure. Negative-FCF issuers go to H, not D.
2. Pull data in this order of preference: Capital IQ consensus via the S&P connector for NTM OCF/FCF/capex/margins; stockanalysis Statistics and Forecast pages for fwd P/E, PEG, 3Y CAGRs, next-FY estimates, PT and analyst count; company guidance only as a fallback, flagged. Non-US listings are screenable through stockanalysis quote pages (e.g. `quote/krx/005930`). Search for the latest print vs consensus separately — #19 can't be run from a statistics page.
3. For A–E, run A → B → C → D and stop that route at the first A fail. Record its reason; do not waive it. If the business belongs to G/H/M/D, run that entire route independently and disclose which core tests it substitutes; a first-failure roster entry never proves the other gates passed. F follows F1–F5; FT applies its additional checks without a lenient-route bypass.
4. Run #19 on the most recent print (revenue vs consensus, adjusted EPS/EBITDA vs consensus, own-guidance low end or midpoint if no range, next-quarter guide vs consensus), using current materiality. A documented #19(d) guidance exemption affects only that guide leg; record both full-year organic-revenue/FCF consensus comparisons and the four-quarter joint-beat history. Actual-result/own-guidance misses remain binding. H uses its documented revenue, cash-burn and milestone substitutions when earnings comparisons are meaningless; F uses F3's sector metrics. If the next print is inside five trading days, re-test after it, not before.
5. Classify A–E as **core**, **conditional core** (named missing evidence, zero capital), **waiver** (#10), **ballast** (only #12 fails), **starter** (#14a), **unresolved starter**, **alert**, **blackout** or **out**. Separately classify **G-compounder/G-watch**, **H-early/H-watch**, **M-platform/M-watch** and **D-growth/D-watch**; watch means zero new capital. Append the PH verdict: warning, impaired, recovery or unverified when applicable; PH-impaired/unverified blocks capital and removes the name from the investable ranking; first apply PH's permitted <1.5%-dividend-yield price-return fallback to eligible data gaps and verify its 10-close/trend/3-or-6-month recovery conjunction. H is speculative, never core before actual graduation evidence. Fintech shortfalls receive no funded starter. Treat minor misses as watch items; missing required evidence is unresolved rather than silently passed.
6. Before capital: verify written #15 company/factor triggers and all simultaneous cap headroom. For A–E/G/H/M/D, #16 name caps remain 25% of sleeve at cost AND 10% of portfolio; AI-capex exposure is ≤60% of sleeve at cost AND ≤25% of portfolio. At 40% of sleeve at market, trim a name back to 32.5% at the next re-test. #14a starters use ≤5.0% sleeve AND ≤1.25% portfolio per name, max three/≤12.5% sleeve aggregate. G initial ≤5.0%/1.25%, total/name ≤12.5%/3.0%, max three/aggregate ≤25%/6.0%; H initial ≤2.5%/0.60%, total/name ≤5.0%/1.25%, max two/aggregate ≤10%/2.5%; M initial ≤2.5%/0.60%, total/name ≤5.0%/1.25%, max two/aggregate ≤10%/2.5%; D initial ≤2.5%/0.60%, total/name ≤5.0%/1.25%, max two/aggregate ≤10%/2.5%. Percent pairs mean sleeve at cost AND total portfolio. Combined #14a/G/H/M/D plus new-entry PH-recovery exposure ≤35% sleeve at cost AND ≤8% portfolio, counted once. Existing verified A–E Core PH-recovery holdings are excluded from that non-core budget, retain PH addition staging/freezes, and still count fully toward ordinary name/factor limits; existing G/H/M/D/starters do not gain this exclusion. PH initial staging ≤5.0%/1.25% or the smaller route initial cap. F retains its separate caps/F5 accounting. Unknown headroom blocks capital. Every addition must satisfy its route's print/checkpoint conditions; route switches retain actual qualification, cost basis and tranche history.
7. Rank verified, PH-eligible core/waiver/ballast by gate strength and asymmetry; show G compounders, capped starters, M platforms, D dilutive growers and speculative H names separately, then unresolved/watch names. Quote 3-year returns only with framework C's return-estimate method (forward EPS base, no separate buyback add, capped exit multiple); the primary ranked list uses its ≥20% base-case guardrail. Show PH-impaired names as turnaround watch items with numeric recovery conditions. Apply the user's lower fintech priority among otherwise comparable qualifiers. Don't rank an unverified projection above verified evidence. State what unlocks or expands capital, and distinguish eligibility from a trade instruction.
8. Update `roster.md` with the date and a "what changed" block. Never change a rule inside a screen — propose it in `changelog.md` under #18.

## Things that go wrong (learned the hard way)

- "Next-FY" is the fiscal year after the last completed one. A June-FYE company reporting in July has FY+1 as next-FY, not FY+2. Check the forecast table's period-ending dates.
- The stockanalysis Forecast table's historical "Free Cash Flow" row uses a narrower definition than OCF − cash PP&E (it ran ~$1B/yr low for KLAC, roughly dividends paid). Use the Statistics page or the company's cash-flow statement for trailing FCF, and don't compare the FY+1 estimate to the forecast row's history 1:1.
- Post-split numbers: KLAC (10:1, Jun 2026) and LRCX (10:1, Oct 2024) report small per-share figures. Sanity-check EPS against revenue and share count before calling a miss.
- Convert-for-equity exchanges, financing/survival issuance and transformative stock deals remain core A1 failures. The ≤0.50% aggregate bolt-on/acqui-hire/share-earnout carve-out requires maximum pending-share accounting AND the separate total diluted-WASO ceiling; it never erases a stricter #15 trigger. G/H/D require their disclosed maximum dilution, funding and per-share tests; issuance alone never establishes qualification there. D measures dilution forward from today's fully diluted count, so price-driven convert dilution must be computed under each instrument's settlement terms, not read off a trailing WASO line.
- JV-funded fabs (SNDK/Kioxia) keep cash PP&E artificially low. The gate stays cash PP&E; JV commitments and guarantees go into #4 at disclosed maximum and JV-inclusive capex goes into #15.
- #11 has two complete core alternatives; verify all prerequisites of one path. #14a permits one bounded shortfall on one applicable path, not blended thresholds or simultaneous growth/valuation exceptions.
- Conflicting forward P/E across sources (AMD: 42.6 vs 32.5; VRT: 33–43): stockanalysis governs. Say so rather than picking the friendlier number.
- For jurisdictions without 10b5-1, verify insider trades through applicable local and SEC filings; missing insider evidence remains unfunded. Read transaction codes and footnotes: required sell-to-cover, issuer withholding and gifts are not discretionary conviction signals. A7's explicit minor-sale missing-footnote fallback may pass with a watch note only after its ≤0.15% annual net-selling and ≤15% C-suite quarterly-disposal conditions are verified; unavailable raw footnotes alone need not block that case. Unknown annual totals/holdings or known sale clusters are not waived.
- A big pullback is not an entry signal and a big run is not an exit signal. Price enters core #11/#12, #14a's narrow band, G/H's per-share valuation, M's OCF-yield valuation, D's dilution-restated return, PH's sustained underperformance/recovery checks and #16's trim rule. For early businesses, contracts, cash runway and achieved milestones matter more than sector enthusiasm. Never give an impaired multi-year chart a clean capital verdict from fundamentals alone.
- When the user pastes someone else's table, verify the numbers before ranking on them; two of the last three pastes mislabeled next-FY growth or used an unsourced sector figure.

## Output format

Lead with the verdict, then the gate detail, then the ranking. Keep it phone-readable.

```
**[TICKER / COMPANY] — [route + status + PH]** One-line verdict. Why it could work; verified facts versus forecasts; material failures or watch items; numeric entry ceiling and eligible size; what unlocks capital. PH: ATH date/age, drawdown, benchmark return gap and recovery checks. For H: funded runway, maximum dilution, next milestone and fixed quality deadline. For D: fully diluted share baseline, projected dilution path, every trailing-12-month raise, D return and stress, HWM yardstick. For FT: normalized cash/risk check. Next catalyst.

**Ranking (gate strength first, then C-metrics)**
1. TICKER — status; 2–3 numbers that decide the slot.
...

**Starters / watchlist:** ticker (shortfall, eligible size or missing evidence, numeric next step), ...
**Compounders / early transitions / platforms / dilutive growth:** ticker (G/H/M/D verdict and route-specific evidence), ...
**Outside the list:** ticker (material gate that fails), ...
Standing caveat (concentration, shared trigger) if relevant.
```

Cite the figures you pulled. Never say a name "decided" or "chose" anything based on a prior assistant suggestion — check the human's own words in past chats before attributing a decision.
