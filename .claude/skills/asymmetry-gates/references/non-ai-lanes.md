# Non-AI lanes: the industry map for non-AI screens (Oct 25, 2026)

Use this file whenever the user asks for a non-AI screen. Screen every lane below, add new names found in each, then apply the normal gates and routes from `framework.md`. Verdicts live in `roster.md`; this file holds the universe.

## What counts as non-AI

- Out of scope: businesses whose growth comes mainly from AI capital spending.
  - Semiconductors, chip equipment, networking and optics.
  - Data centers, servers, power, grid and cooling built for data centers.
  - GPU clouds and hyperscalers.
- In scope: companies that only use AI in their product.
- A8 still applies to them: a third party that can route around the company (e.g. AI answers replacing visits) sends it to #10 unless the user reads A8 as neutral with a #15 trigger.
- Floors: market cap ≥$2.5B and ≥$25M average daily dollar volume.
- Banks and insurers: logged, not screened (NU, KNSL, KSPI, AXP).
- Routing:
  - Fintech, payments, brokers and crypto go through the FT overlay (`fintech.md`).
  - Commodity producers and power generators go to F.
