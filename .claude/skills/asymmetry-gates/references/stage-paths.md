# G/H — Compounders and early transitions (Oct 3, 2026, revision 4)

Read for a durable-theme business that fails the strict A–E route or for a business still building its cash engine. These are separate qualification paths, not ticker waivers or #14 near-passes. Apply the fintech overlay first; fintech cannot enter G/H. Levered power/IPPs and commodity names remain F-only.

G replaces core A1/A2, A3(b), A5's ranking/margin formulation, A7's officer-count signal and #9/#11/#12 with G1–G5; it preserves the other cash, leverage, end-market, disintermediation and legal protections as specified below. H replaces current A1–A5 and earnings/valuation assumptions with H1–H5, preserving A6/A8 through evidenced demand and competitive-chain position, and using H5 for pre-profit insider/legal comparisons. The selected route's hard failures govern #17 reviews; an H company is not exited simply because core A3/A4 still fail before graduation.

A theme directs research. Demand, competitive advantage, per-share economics, funding and entry valuation decide eligibility. A rising sector or a large TAM does not establish that a stock will rise indefinitely. Prefer A–E when a name actually qualifies there; do not force a profitable company into the early route because it has an expensive multiple.

## G. Proven compounder

All G tests must be verified. Missing evidence means **G-watch**, zero new capital; a verified failure means **out of G**. A passing name is **G-compounder**, separate from core.

- **G1 — Business evidence.** At least two quantified, dated indicators of a durable competitive position: retention/renewals, recurring revenue, firm backlog conversion, market share in a defined segment, pricing power or installed-base/service economics. Explain the causal connection between the theme and revenue/cash flow; distinguish organic demand from acquisitions and optional orders. Preserve A6/A8: a growing end-market and no unresolved third-party bypass risk. Top-two share is not required where defensible niche leadership is evidenced.
- **G2 — Existing cash engine.** Company FCF = OCF minus all cash PP&E, without adding back acquisition costs or SBC cash effects. Trailing FCF margin ≥6%, positive OCF in each of the last two completed FYs, NTM FCF ≥90% of trailing FCF, capex ≤50% of NTM OCF, and pro forma net debt/NTM FCF ≤2.5× using A4's maximum-exposure accounting. A forecast of profits cannot replace this actual floor. Next-FY organic revenue growth ≥10%, forward 3Y organic revenue CAGR ≥10% and modeled 3Y fully diluted FCF/share CAGR ≥12%. Explain margin changes using segment and operating economics; unexplained deterioration is unresolved.
- **G3 — Capital allocation and dilution.** Buybacks are optional. Ordinary net dilution, excluding separately reconciled acquisition consideration, ≤3.5% YoY (rev 4); use the higher of diluted WASO and period-end economic share-count growth on a comparable split-adjusted basis. Include SBC, converts, warrants, earnouts and announced deals in projected fully diluted shares. An acquisition or convertible issuance is not an automatic rejection: establish its purpose, maximum dilution, cash/debt obligations and funding. For a stock-funded deal, base-case FCF/share must recover to at least the pre-deal standalone level within 24 months, with pro forma G2 leverage intact. Unknown conversion/earnout exposure, survival financing or reliance on an uncommitted future raise means no entry. Show per-share outcomes in bear/base/bull; revenue growth alone cannot justify dilution.
- **G4 — Insiders and legal exposure.** Retain A7's ≤0.5%-of-shares annual net-sales limit, verified local disclosures and regulatory vetoes. Use the framework's mandatory sell-to-cover reading. Three officers making discretionary non-plan sales is a watch flag requiring documented holdings and sale context, rather than an automatic G rejection. Reject G if at least three officers each dispose of >25% of their pre-sale economic holdings through such sales within the same rolling 90 days. Unknown holdings or trade purpose remain G-watch. A small count-based flag never excuses excessive aggregate selling or a regulatory failure.
- **G5 — Price and durability.** Replace #11/#12 with a sourced five-year bear/base/bull model of revenue, operating conversion, capex, debt and fully diluted shares. Model cash FCF/share, not adjusted EPS alone. Base-case annualized total return ≥10% (rev 4); operating bear-case year-five share value ≥60% of today's entry price. Terminal multiples cannot exceed the lower of the company's five-year median and comparable profitable peers without a separately justified lower entry ceiling. Include acquisition integration, competition and margin normalization; label assumptions and coverage. If fewer than five analysts cover a future period, use disclosed operating evidence and explicitly flagged estimates rather than treating a company target as consensus. Missing support means G-watch. Report the numeric entry-price ceiling implied by the model.
- **G6 — Entry, size and review.** Current #13 timing, #19 materiality and written #15 triggers apply. Initial entry ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.5% of portfolio; total G exposure per name ≤5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤1% of portfolio. Max three G names, aggregate ≤15% of sleeve at cost AND ≤3% of portfolio. Add only after a subsequent print verifies G1–G5 and #19, inside the combined budget below. Review quarterly under #17. Exit on a verified cash/solvency/dilution/governance failure in G2–G4, loss of the documented G1 competitive position/A8 protection, or a written exit. Forecast growth or valuation shortfalls freeze additions and prompt review rather than automatic exits. Promotion to core requires actual full A–E qualification, preserving cost basis and tranche history.

## H. Early transition

Use when a non-fintech operating business lacks the current cash engine but has demonstrable technology/product delivery and a credible path to a defined quality destination. A passing name is **H-early**; it is speculative and never described as core or certain to succeed. Missing evidence means **H-watch**, zero capital. A verified critical failure means **out of H**.

- **H1 — Demonstrated progress and demand.** At least one independently verifiable technical/product milestone already achieved, plus either realized commercial revenue or a binding customer commitment with specified consideration and delivery obligations. Record cancellation rights, optional amounts and revenue-recognition timing. MOUs, market size, optional backlog and management enthusiasm do not count as binding demand. Explain unit economics and a concrete route from delivery to repeatable gross profit, supported by tests/contracts rather than market-share assumptions alone.
- **H2 — Funded execution.** Unrestricted cash, liquid investments and genuinely available committed facilities must fund the next 24 months of modeled net cash consumption, cash PP&E and due debt/lease/buildout/deal obligations, with a 25% cash-consumption overrun buffer. Avoid double-counting obligations already in burn; show the reconciliation. Exclude restricted/client cash, unopened ATM capacity, optional contracts and uncommitted financing. Later raises may appear in the five-year model at conservative issue prices with explicit dilution; they cannot be counted as funded liquidity today. Model lower/slower revenue, not just current burn extrapolated through a growth ramp. Going-concern dependence on an uncommitted raise means H-watch.
- **H3 — Fixed quality destination.** At entry set a dated deadline within five years for positive company FCF, FCF margin ≥6% and full qualification for either A–E or G; identify the destination and map every presently failed test to its numeric target. Show annual revenue, operating margins, cash capex, working capital, financing and fully diluted shares from now to that deadline. Base-case ordinary dilution must converge to the destination's limit. Do not omit satellite launches, factories, maintenance spend, customer acquisition costs or acquisition commitments. EBITDA profitability alone is insufficient. Keep the original clock; resetting the deadline at each review cannot preserve eligibility. If only the bull case reaches the destination, H-watch.
- **H4 — Valuation and ownership.** Replace current P/E/PEG/FCF-yield tests with five-year diluted per-share bear/base/bull values. Base-case annualized total return ≥18% (rev 4) after all financing and dilution; state the numeric entry-price ceiling. Use profitable comparable businesses and restrained terminal multiples, never a TAM multiple. Include a failed-commercialization case with zero equity value and show the loss at the proposed position size. Write a numeric maximum fully diluted share count for the first 24 months and for the quality deadline, tied to the funding plan; unexplained or exceeded dilution invalidates the path. A funding round can be sensible and still make the current entry price unattractive.
- **H5 — Governance and entry comparisons.** Verify insiders using A7's net-sales limit and G4's cluster test; the mandatory sell-to-cover reading still applies. Structural-remedy orders remain a veto. Where NTM FCF is non-positive, replace A7's loss/FCF denominator with a disclosed legal-loss stress: deduct the maximum disclosed reasonably-possible loss from usable liquidity and rerun H2; unknown material exposure is H-watch. For #19, use revenue consensus when meaningful and available, own revenue/gross-profit/cash-burn guidance, and the pre-recorded delivery milestones. Missing or meaningless adjusted EPS/EBITDA consensus need not block H when the substituted metrics are documented before entry. Revenue vs consensus retains #19's >2% test, and next-period revenue guide vs meaningful consensus retains its >3% test. Revenue/gross profit below own guided low end (or >1% below own midpoint when no range), cash burn >20% above the guided/model ceiling, or an invalidating technical/commercial failure blocks entry and freezes additions pending H review. Do not call a missed critical milestone a pass because EPS is irrelevant. #13's earnings-within-five-trading-days pause remains.
- **H6 — Small, earned exposure.** Initial entry ≤1.25% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.25% of portfolio. Total per name ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.5% of portfolio; max two names, aggregate ≤5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤1% of portfolio. No 5%-minimum target. Before entry write numeric, dated 6/12/24-month technical, commercialization, cash-burn and dilution checkpoints in `triggers.md`; write company-specific exit conditions. Add only after an achieved checkpoint and a fresh full H re-test. A price drop or stronger theme is not a checkpoint.
- **H7 — Failure and graduation.** Review every print and major launch/test, funding round or contract change. Stress runway <18 months freezes additions; <12 months exits. A critical failure that destroys the documented route, a checkpoint >90 days late without an already documented and funded recovery plan, breach of written share-count limits, or loss of the original five-year quality path exits. A recoverable test failure requires a funded, dated recovery plan and no adds until verified recovery. Promote to G or A–E only on actual trailing cash evidence and all destination gates; preserve cost basis, the entry clock and tranche history. Promotion is never earned merely by a higher price or a more optimistic projection.

## Shared valuation and capital accounting

Use one disclosed per-share valuation convention. For a simple five-year comparison: annualized return = ((year-five share value + actual modeled cash dividends through year five) / current entry price)^(1/5) − 1. Retained cash belongs in terminal net cash; do not also count it as a dividend. For enterprise-value models, subtract debt/claims and add available cash before dividing by fully diluted shares. Do not double-count cash FCF as both distributed cash and retained terminal value. Ordinary bear losses above are terminal scenario losses, not guaranteed drawdown limits.

All #14a, G, H and A–E/G/H PH-recovery positions combined ≤20% of sleeve at cost AND ≤4% of total portfolio, counting each position once. Their individual/route caps still apply. G/H count toward #16 name limits and relevant shared-factor caps; they do not create another unrestricted sleeve. PH also applies to both routes and can block additions despite a G/H pass; short history is handled under `price-health.md`. A name uses one entry path at a time, and a route switch preserves cost basis. Unknown portfolio headroom means no capital eligibility. Existing core holdings do not become forced sales solely because a route label changes.

These horizons, return hurdles and caps express the user's preferences; they are not backtested forecasts or promises of eventual qualification.
