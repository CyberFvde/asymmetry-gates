# G/H/M/D — Compounders, early transitions, mega-cap platforms and dilutive growth (Oct 24, 2026, revision 7)

Read for a durable-theme business that fails the strict A–E route, for a business still building its cash engine, for a mega-cap platform whose company FCF is temporarily depressed by growth capex (M, added in revision 6), or for a self-funding high-growth business that fails core A1 or G3 on equity issuance but still clears a high per-share return after all dilution (D, added in revision 7). These are separate qualification paths, not ticker waivers or #14 near-passes. Apply the fintech overlay first; fintech cannot enter G/H/M/D. Levered power/IPPs and commodity names remain F-only. G and H text is unchanged from revision 5; M is unchanged from revision 6.

G replaces core A1/A2, A3(b), A5's ranking/margin formulation, A7's officer-count signal and #9/#11/#12 with G1–G5; it preserves the other cash, leverage, end-market, disintermediation and legal protections as specified below. H replaces current A1–A5 and earnings/valuation assumptions with H1–H5, preserving A6/A8 through evidenced demand and competitive-chain position, and using H5 for pre-profit insider/legal comparisons. The selected route's hard failures govern #17 reviews; an H company is not exited simply because core A3/A4 still fail before graduation.

A theme directs research. Demand, competitive advantage, per-share economics, funding and entry valuation decide eligibility. A rising sector or a large TAM does not establish that a stock will rise indefinitely. Prefer A–E when a name actually qualifies there; do not force a profitable company into the early route because it has an expensive multiple.

## G. Proven compounder

All G tests must be verified. Missing evidence means **G-watch**, zero new capital; a verified failure means **out of G**. A passing name is **G-compounder**, separate from core.

- **G1 — Business evidence.** At least two quantified, dated indicators of a durable competitive position: retention/renewals, recurring revenue, firm backlog conversion, market share in a defined segment, pricing power or installed-base/service economics. Explain the causal connection between the theme and revenue/cash flow; distinguish organic demand from acquisitions and optional orders. Preserve A6/A8: a growing end-market and no unresolved third-party bypass risk. Top-two share is not required where defensible niche leadership is evidenced.
- **G2 — Existing cash engine.** Company FCF = OCF minus all cash PP&E, without adding back acquisition costs or SBC cash effects. Trailing FCF margin ≥6%, positive OCF in each of the last two completed FYs, NTM FCF ≥90% of trailing FCF, capex ≤50% of NTM OCF, and pro forma net debt/NTM FCF ≤2.5× using A4's maximum-exposure accounting. A forecast of profits cannot replace this actual floor. Next-FY organic revenue growth ≥10%, forward 3Y organic revenue CAGR ≥10% and modeled 3Y fully diluted FCF/share CAGR ≥12%. Explain margin changes using segment and operating economics; unexplained deterioration is unresolved.
- **G3 — Capital allocation and dilution.** Buybacks are optional. Ordinary net dilution, excluding separately reconciled acquisition consideration, ≤3% YoY; use the higher of diluted WASO and period-end economic share-count growth on a comparable split-adjusted basis. Include SBC, converts, warrants, earnouts and announced deals in projected fully diluted shares. An acquisition or convertible issuance is not an automatic rejection: establish its purpose, maximum dilution, cash/debt obligations and funding. For a stock-funded deal, base-case FCF/share must recover to at least the pre-deal standalone level within 24 months, with pro forma G2 leverage intact. Unknown conversion/earnout exposure, survival financing or reliance on an uncommitted future raise means no entry. Show per-share outcomes in bear/base/bull; revenue growth alone cannot justify dilution.
- **G4 — Insiders and legal exposure.** Retain A7's ≤0.5%-of-shares annual net-sales limit, verified local disclosures and regulatory vetoes. Use the framework's mandatory sell-to-cover reading. Three officers making discretionary non-plan sales is a watch flag requiring documented holdings and sale context, rather than an automatic G rejection. Reject G if at least three officers each dispose of >25% of their pre-sale economic holdings through such sales within the same rolling 90 days. Unknown holdings remain G-watch. Unknown trade purpose remains G-watch except for the framework's bounded A7 missing-Form-4-footnote pass-with-watch when every condition is verified; it does not waive this route's separate cluster/holdings or legal checks. A small count-based flag never excuses excessive aggregate selling or a regulatory failure.
- **G5 — Price and durability.** Replace #11/#12 with a sourced five-year bear/base/bull model of revenue, operating conversion, capex, debt and fully diluted shares. Model cash FCF/share, not adjusted EPS alone. Base-case annualized total return ≥12%; operating bear-case year-five share value ≥60% of today's entry price. Terminal multiples cannot exceed the lower of the company's five-year median and comparable profitable peers without a separately justified lower entry ceiling. Include acquisition integration, competition and margin normalization; label assumptions and coverage. If fewer than five analysts cover a future period, use disclosed operating evidence and explicitly flagged estimates rather than treating a company target as consensus. Missing support means G-watch. Report the numeric entry-price ceiling implied by the model.
- **G6 — Entry, size and review.** Current #13 timing, #19 materiality and written #15 triggers apply. Initial entry ≤5.0% of sleeve at cost AND ≤1.25% of portfolio; total G exposure per name ≤12.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤3.0% of portfolio. Max three G names, aggregate ≤25% of sleeve at cost AND ≤6.0% of portfolio. Add only after a subsequent print verifies G1–G5 and #19, inside the combined budget below. Review quarterly under #17. Exit on a verified cash/solvency/dilution/governance failure in G2–G4, loss of the documented G1 competitive position/A8 protection, or a written exit. Forecast growth or valuation shortfalls freeze additions and prompt review rather than automatic exits. Promotion to core requires actual full A–E qualification, preserving cost basis and tranche history.

## H. Early transition

Use when a non-fintech operating business lacks the current cash engine but has demonstrable technology/product delivery and a credible path to a defined quality destination. **Mandatory check (Oct 24, 2026):** every non-FT, non-F name that fails A–E, G, M and D gets an H review before it is called out, recorded as H-early, H-watch, out of H (naming the deciding test) or H not applicable (with the reason). The check adds a review, not a lower bar. A passing name is **H-early**; it is speculative and never described as core or certain to succeed. Missing evidence means **H-watch**, zero capital. A verified critical failure means **out of H**.

- **H1 — Demonstrated progress and demand.** At least one independently verifiable technical/product milestone already achieved, plus either realized commercial revenue or a binding customer commitment with specified consideration and delivery obligations. Record cancellation rights, optional amounts and revenue-recognition timing. MOUs, market size, optional backlog and management enthusiasm do not count as binding demand. Explain unit economics and a concrete route from delivery to repeatable gross profit, supported by tests/contracts rather than market-share assumptions alone.
- **H2 — Funded execution.** Unrestricted cash, liquid investments and genuinely available committed facilities must fund the next 24 months of modeled net cash consumption, cash PP&E and due debt/lease/buildout/deal obligations, with a 25% cash-consumption overrun buffer. Avoid double-counting obligations already in burn; show the reconciliation. Exclude restricted/client cash, unopened ATM capacity, optional contracts and uncommitted financing. Later raises may appear in the five-year model at conservative issue prices with explicit dilution; they cannot be counted as funded liquidity today. Model lower/slower revenue, not just current burn extrapolated through a growth ramp. Going-concern dependence on an uncommitted raise means H-watch.
- **H3 — Fixed quality destination.** At entry set a dated deadline within five years for positive company FCF, FCF margin ≥6% and full qualification for either A–E or G; identify the destination and map every presently failed test to its numeric target. Show annual revenue, operating margins, cash capex, working capital, financing and fully diluted shares from now to that deadline. Base-case ordinary dilution must converge to the destination's limit. Do not omit satellite launches, factories, maintenance spend, customer acquisition costs or acquisition commitments. EBITDA profitability alone is insufficient. Keep the original clock; resetting the deadline at each review cannot preserve eligibility. If only the bull case reaches the destination, H-watch.
- **H4 — Valuation and ownership.** Replace current P/E/PEG/FCF-yield tests with five-year diluted per-share bear/base/bull values. Base-case annualized total return ≥20% after all financing and dilution; state the numeric entry-price ceiling. Use profitable comparable businesses and restrained terminal multiples, never a TAM multiple. Include a failed-commercialization case with zero equity value and show the loss at the proposed position size. Write a numeric maximum fully diluted share count for the first 24 months and for the quality deadline, tied to the funding plan; unexplained or exceeded dilution invalidates the path. A funding round can be sensible and still make the current entry price unattractive.
- **H5 — Governance and entry comparisons.** Verify insiders using A7's net-sales limit and G4's cluster test; the mandatory sell-to-cover reading and bounded missing-footnote fallback apply only as specified in A7, without waiving H5/G4 holdings, legal or funding checks. Structural-remedy orders remain a veto. Where NTM FCF is non-positive, replace A7's loss/FCF denominator with a disclosed legal-loss stress: deduct the maximum disclosed reasonably-possible loss from usable liquidity and rerun H2; unknown material exposure is H-watch. For #19, use revenue consensus when meaningful and available, own revenue/gross-profit/cash-burn guidance, and the pre-recorded delivery milestones. Missing or meaningless adjusted EPS/EBITDA consensus need not block H when the substituted metrics are documented before entry. Revenue vs consensus retains #19's >2% test, and next-period revenue guide vs meaningful consensus retains its >3% test. The #19(d) exemption may make only that guide comparison watch-only if every applicable full-year organic-revenue/FCF and paired-beat-history condition is actually verified; H's present cash exemption cannot substitute for those facts. Revenue/gross profit below own guided low end (or >1% below own midpoint when no range), cash burn >20% above the guided/model ceiling, or an invalidating technical/commercial failure blocks entry and freezes additions pending H review. Do not call a missed critical milestone a pass because EPS is irrelevant. #13's earnings-within-five-trading-days pause remains.
- **H6 — Small, earned exposure.** Initial entry ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.60% of portfolio. Total per name ≤5.0% of sleeve at cost AND ≤1.25% of portfolio; max two names, aggregate ≤10% of sleeve at cost AND ≤2.5% of portfolio. No 5%-minimum target. Before entry write numeric, dated 6/12/24-month technical, commercialization, cash-burn and dilution checkpoints in `triggers.md`; write company-specific exit conditions. Add only after an achieved checkpoint and a fresh full H re-test. A price drop or stronger theme is not a checkpoint.
- **H7 — Failure and graduation.** Review every print and major launch/test, funding round or contract change. Stress runway <18 months freezes additions; <12 months exits. A critical failure that destroys the documented route, a checkpoint >90 days late without an already documented and funded recovery plan, breach of written share-count limits, or loss of the original five-year quality path exits. A recoverable test failure requires a funded, dated recovery plan and no adds until verified recovery. Promote to G or A–E only on actual trailing cash evidence and all destination gates; preserve cost basis, the entry clock and tranche history. Promotion is never earned merely by a higher price or a more optimistic projection.

## M. Mega-cap platform in a reinvestment cycle (revision 6)

Use for a very large, cash-generative platform that fails core A2/A3 because growth capex has pushed company FCF down. M is a capped class route, not a core pass, a #14 near-pass or a one-name waiver. All M tests must be verified. Missing evidence means **M-watch**, zero new capital; a verified failure means **out of M**; a passing name is **M-platform**, shown separately from core.

M replaces A1's WASO ceiling (M1: ≤ +1.5% without requiring buybacks, a loosening versus core), A2 (buybacks/net cash), A3(a)–(c) and 3a, A4's FCF denominator, #9 and #11 with M1–M5. M5 keeps Standard Core's P/E and PEG limits and swaps its FCF-yield component for OCF yield; there is no high-velocity path and no #14a band. M keeps A1's issuance bans and de minimis accounting, A5, A6, A7 (with M2(e)'s loss denominator), A8, #12, #13, #15, #17, #19 and PH. Prefer A–E or 3a when a name qualifies there.

- **M0. Scope.** Market cap ≥$500B at the screen date; revenue up in each of the last five completed FYs. FT and F names cannot use M. A PH-impaired name needs verified PH-recovery first.
- **M1. Issuance and dilution.** A1's financing/survival/transformative issuance bans and its ≤0.50% de minimis acquisition carve-out apply unchanged; unknown maximum deal shares means M-watch. Diluted WASO ≤ +1.5% YoY on a split-adjusted basis (A1's ceiling for buyback-active growers). Buybacks are optional.
- **M2. Cash engine.** (a) Trailing-4Q OCF margin ≥20%. (b) Positive OCF in each of the last five completed FYs, and trailing-4Q OCF ≥ last completed FY OCF. (c) Guided/consensus NTM cash capex ≤125% of NTM OCF; vague guidance defaults to trailing cash PP&E ×1.25. (d) Recovery: consensus company FCF margin ≥6% (A3(a)'s floor) in FY+2, the second fiscal year after the last completed FY (for a December filer screened in 2026, FY2027), from ≥5 analysts; fewer analysts or no FCF consensus means M-watch. Before entry the test year rolls forward with each completed FY; at entry, record the recovery year as a fixed clock. (e) A7's reasonably-possible-loss test uses 5% of NTM OCF in place of NTM FCF.
- **M3. Leverage.** Net debt including finance and operating lease liabilities, guarantees, vendor financing and pending deal consideration at maximum exposure ≤1.0× trailing-4Q EBITDA (operating income + D&A). Lease and vendor-financing figures go in #15.
- **M4. Growth.** Forward 3Y revenue CAGR ≥10% AND next-FY organic revenue growth ≥10%, replacing #9. No back-end-loaded hockey sticks.
- **M5. Valuation.** Fwd P/E ≤38 AND PEG ≤2.0 AND NTM OCF yield ≥4.0% (P/OCF ≤25×), replacing #11 (Standard Core limits with OCF yield in place of FCF yield). #14a bands do not apply. #12 applies unchanged; its FCF-yield leg is unavailable while NTM FCF yield is below 5%.
- **M6. Size and accounting.** Initial entry ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.60% of portfolio; total per name ≤5.0% of sleeve AND ≤1.25% of portfolio; max two M names, aggregate ≤10% of sleeve at cost AND ≤2.5% of portfolio. M counts inside the combined non-core budget below and in #16's name caps. A name whose capex is mainly data-center/AI infrastructure carries the full shared "AI capex 2027" trigger (tripwire B is its hyperscaler leg) and counts in its 60%/25% group cap. Add only after the next print clears #19 and a fresh M re-test.
- **M7. Exits and graduation.** Write #15 triggers at entry covering OCF margin, M3 leverage, capex/OCF and the recovery-year FCF consensus. Out of M on: any financing/survival/transformative issuance; trailing-4Q OCF margin <18%; M3 leverage >1.5×; recovery-year actual FCF margin <6% when that year is reported (no clock reset); consensus recovery-year FCF margin <3% at two consecutive re-tests; or two consecutive prints with a material #19 trigger. Other shortfalls freeze additions and prompt a re-test. Promotion to core requires actual full A–E qualification, including A3(a)'s actual FCF floor, preserving cost basis and tranche history.

## D. Dilutive growth (revision 7)

Use for a high-growth operating business that already funds itself from operations but fails core A1 (or G3) because it issued equity or equity-linked securities, or settled legacy convertibles in stock. The question D asks is the one a buyer today faces: after every share that exists or is likely to exist, does the per-share return still clear a high bar? D is a capped class route, not a core pass, a #14 near-pass or a one-name waiver. All D tests must be verified. Missing evidence means **D-watch**, zero new capital; a verified failure means **out of D**; a passing name is **D-growth**, shown separately from core.

D replaces A1's WASO ceiling and trailing-12-month issuance ban, A2 (buybacks/net cash), A3(b)/(c), #9 and #11 with D1–D5. It keeps A3(a)'s actual FCF floor (inside D2), A4 (as D3), A5, A6, A7, A8, #12, #13, #15, #17, #19 and PH. Prefer A–E, G or M when a name qualifies there. A business with negative trailing FCF can use D only through D2(c)'s capex-cycle quality branch, which asks for the profile of an established, profitable platform in a build-out (the user's benchmark for quality is GOOGL-type economics); anything else belongs in H, whose funding, runway and fixed-destination tests are built for it.

- **D0. Scope.** Non-FT, non-F operating company with market cap ≥$10B at the screen date; smaller dilutive issuers use H. A PH-impaired name needs verified PH-recovery first.
- **D1. Dilution, measured forward.** (a) **Baseline:** today's fully diluted share count (FDSO) = basic shares + treasury-stock-method awards + every convertible note, convertible preferred and warrant valued at the screen-date price under its contractual settlement method (net-share settlement adds only the excess conversion value in shares; physical settlement adds every conversion share). A capped call or note hedge offsets only up to its cap; issuer warrants add shares above their strike. Legacy converts already settled or exchanged sit in basic shares, so trailing dilution is sunk and is not re-tested here; it still shows in D1(c) and in A7/#15 history. (b) **Projected dilution:** model FDSO for each of the next three fiscal years from SBC at the trailing run rate less committed buybacks, every convert/preferred/warrant at the base-case year-end price, announced or pending issuance at maximum, and the trailing-12-month ATM pace unless the program is terminated. Base-case FDSO growth ≤8% a year on average and ≤10% in any single year. (c) **Raise test:** every equity or equity-linked issuance in the trailing 12 months, or announced/pending, must be non-survival (cash, liquid investments and committed facilities covered the next 24 months of the base plan without it), must fund capex or working capital for contracted/backlog demand, refinance debt, fund buybacks, or pay acquisition consideration that meets G3's stock-deal test (maximum dilution counted, base-case FCF/share back to the pre-deal standalone level within 24 months, pro forma A4 intact), and a new convert or convertible preferred must carry a conversion premium ≥30% at pricing. **Strategic placements (P14, adopted Oct 24):** a sale of common stock, or of preferred that converts 1:1 into common with no coupon, reset or liquidation preference above common, to an operating customer or supplier at or above the 5-day VWAP, for disclosed capex or capacity, is tested like a placement (purpose and non-survival) without the premium requirement; a discount, attached warrants or survival purpose still fails. ATM proceeds over the trailing 12 months ≤5% of average market cap. Unknown conversion terms, settlement method or maximum pending shares mean D-watch.
- **D2. Cash engine.** Pass (a)+(b), or (c). (a) Trailing-4Q company FCF margin ≥6% (A3(a)'s floor), OR trailing-4Q company FCF positive with consensus FY+1 FCF margin ≥6% from ≥5 analysts; in that case record FY+1 as a fixed recovery year at entry. (b) Consensus NTM company FCF >0. No separate capex/OCF ceiling: the FCF floors already net out all cash PP&E, and capex/OCF goes into #15. (c) **Capex-cycle quality branch** (user request, Oct 24): trailing or NTM FCF may be negative only when growth capex causes it and every one of these holds: (1) trailing-4Q OCF margin ≥20% AND trailing-4Q GAAP operating income >0; (2) OCF positive in each of the last two completed FYs; (3) consensus company FCF margin ≥6% in FY+2 (the second FY after the last completed FY) from ≥5 analysts, recorded at entry as a fixed recovery year; (4) unrestricted cash, liquid investments and undrawn committed facilities fund the next 24 months of cash capex minus OCF, plus debt and lease maturities, with a 25% overrun buffer, excluding unopened ATMs, uncommitted raises and restricted cash (H2's accounting); (5) net debt including converts at principal, leases, guarantees and vendor financing at maximum ≤2.5× trailing-4Q EBITDA (operating income + D&A), replacing A4's FCF denominator; D3(b)'s debt-to-equity cap still applies. Customer prepayments count in OCF only as reported; they are not added again as funding. Missing evidence on any item means D-watch.
- **D3. Leverage.** (a) A4 unchanged (a D2(c) name uses D2(c)(5) in its place): net debt including converts at principal, leases, guarantees and vendor financing at maximum exposure ≤2.5× NTM FCF. (b) Book debt-to-equity ≤1.99×: total debt including converts and lease liabilities ÷ total shareholders' equity at the latest balance sheet; stockanalysis Statistics governs, and negative equity fails. Both must pass; issuing stock lowers D/E, so (b) never substitutes for (a) or for D2's cash floor.
- **D4. Growth.** Forward 3Y revenue CAGR ≥25% AND consensus organic revenue growth ≥20% in both next-FY and the fiscal year after it (≥5 analysts). Growth is the reason to accept dilution, so the bar sits above core's 15%/12%.
- **D5. Valuation and return.** (a) Fwd P/E ≤45 AND PEG ≤1.65 (the high-velocity core caps; stockanalysis governs). (b) **D return** = framework C's return estimate with each year's EPS restated as consensus EPS × (consensus share count for that year ÷ D1 projected FDSO) whenever projected FDSO is higher; base case ≥25%. (c) **Stress:** the same model with every forward year's EPS growth cut by 5 points still returns ≥20%. (d) **HWM yardstick** (user-designated opportunity cost): the base-case D return beats HWM's latest recorded framework C estimate by ≥5 points AND the forward 3Y revenue CAGR beats HWM's. Record the HWM figures and their date with each screen. On the Oct 22, 2026 re-test (PR #11, unmerged) HWM was ~15.4%, so the yardstick sits near 20.4% and D's 25% floor binds first. Changing the yardstick name needs a #18 entry.
- **D6. Size and accounting.** Initial entry ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.60% of portfolio; total per name ≤5.0% of sleeve AND ≤1.25% of portfolio; max two D names, of which at most one may enter through D2(c), aggregate ≤10% of sleeve at cost AND ≤2.5% of portfolio. D counts inside the combined non-core budget below and in #16's name and factor caps; a semi/AI-infra D name carries the full shared "AI capex 2027" trigger and counts in its 60%/25% group cap. Add only after the next print clears #19 and a fresh D re-test confirms D1–D5.
- **D7. Exits and graduation.** Write #15 triggers at entry covering the FDSO baseline and the maximum base-case FDSO path for years 1–3, the D2 floors and any recovery year, A4 leverage, the D4 growth line and capex/OCF. **Out of D on:** any issuance failing D1(c); actual FDSO growth over a trailing 4Q above 10%, or above the written maximum path, excluding settlement of instruments already counted in the entry baseline; trailing-4Q FCF margin <3% for a D2(a) name, or the recovery year's actual FCF margin <6% when reported (no clock reset); for a D2(c) name, trailing-4Q OCF margin <15%, GAAP operating income ≤0 for two consecutive quarters, net debt >3.5× trailing EBITDA, stressed funding below 18 months, or consensus recovery-year FCF margin <3% at two consecutive re-tests; net debt >3.0× NTM FCF; book debt-to-equity ≥2.5×; or two consecutive prints with a material #19 trigger. **Freeze additions and re-test on:** D return <20% or stress <15% at a re-test; next-FY organic growth consensus <20%; projected FDSO growth above 8% a year. Promotion to core requires actual full A–E qualification, including A1's own ceilings once its 12-month issuance window rolls off; promotion to G requires full G1–G5. Preserve cost basis and tranche history.

## Shared valuation and capital accounting

Use one disclosed per-share valuation convention. Separable optionality follows framework C's SOTP convention (outside price only, 25% haircut, base/bull only, ≤20% of base-case value, never used for a gate). For a simple five-year comparison: annualized return = ((year-five share value + actual modeled cash dividends through year five) / current entry price)^(1/5) − 1. Retained cash belongs in terminal net cash; do not also count it as a dividend. For enterprise-value models, subtract debt/claims and add available cash before dividing by fully diluted shares. Do not double-count cash FCF as both distributed cash and retained terminal value. Ordinary bear losses above are terminal scenario losses, not guaranteed drawdown limits.

All #14a, G, H, M, D and new-entry A–E/G/H/M/D PH-recovery positions combined ≤35% of sleeve at cost AND ≤8% of total portfolio, counting each position once. Existing verified A–E Core holdings entering PH-recovery are excluded from this non-core budget, but retain PH staging/addition freezes and ordinary name/factor accounting. Existing G/H/M/D/starters remain counted and cannot create headroom by changing labels. Their individual/route caps still apply. G/H/M/D count toward #16 name limits (25% sleeve at cost AND 10% portfolio) and relevant shared-factor caps, including AI-capex ≤60% sleeve at cost AND ≤25% portfolio; they do not create another unrestricted sleeve. PH also applies to all four routes and can block additions despite a G/H/M/D pass; short history is handled under `price-health.md`. A name uses one entry path at a time, and a route switch preserves cost basis. Unknown portfolio headroom means no capital eligibility. Existing core holdings do not become forced sales solely because a route label changes.

These horizons, return hurdles and caps express the user's preferences. The sleeve's 25%+ annualized-return objective is not a new G/H per-name hurdle or a forecast; D's 25% base-case D return is a route-specific hurdle the user chose for dilutive names (revision 7). G3's 3% dilution cap, G5's 12% base-return hurdle and H4's 20% hurdle remain at merged-main values. Larger allocations require the same earned evidence; no backtest or promise of eventual qualification is claimed.
