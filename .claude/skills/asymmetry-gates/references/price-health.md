# PH — Price health and stale highs (Oct 24, 2026, revision 7; route names only)

Read for every equity screen. The user wants prolonged failure to recover an old high to affect eligibility and ranking. Fundamental quality and market performance are separate assessments; both must be visible. A fresh ATH is not required, and a recent rally alone does not qualify a business.

## Measurements

Use the last completed regular-session close and record the data date. Report the split-adjusted **closing ATH**, its date and age, drawdown from that ATH, three-year return and its gap versus a preselected broad benchmark on the total-return or explicitly permitted price-return basis, current close versus the 200-day moving average, today's 200DMA versus its value 20 trading sessions ago, 50DMA versus 200DMA and the prior completed-session 50DMA when using the alternate trend test, and trailing 3-/6-month relative returns for recovery.

Use consistent price/total-return series, dates and currency. ATH/drawdown/200DMA use split-adjusted closes; relative returns normally include reinvested dividends and relevant distribution adjustments; the bounded price-return fallback below is the sole ordinary-dividend exception. Default benchmark: S&P 500 total return for US listings, an appropriate broad local-market total-return benchmark for non-US listings, fixed before seeing the result. State it; do not choose a weaker benchmark to obtain a pass. The return gap is stock total return minus benchmark total return in **percentage points**, not a ratio of percentages. Record both intraday and closing highs only if clearly distinguished; the test uses closing highs.

**Low-dividend price-return fallback (Oct 15):** when free sources provide full split-adjusted price history but not the required dividend-reinvested return history, a stock with verified trailing dividend yield **<1.5%** may use split-adjusted stock price return versus **S&P 500 price return** for the three-year and applicable 3-/6-month relative comparisons. Choose and disclose this return basis before inspecting its outcome; use matching dates and currency (record FX conversion where needed), and do not compare stock price return to benchmark total return. Label the figures price returns, not total returns. Record the trailing yield and source; yield ≥1.5% or unknown does not qualify for this fallback. Ordinary-dividend data unavailability alone need not make an otherwise complete low-yield history PH-unverified. Special distributions, spin-offs/demergers and split adjustments must still be reconciled. ATH/drawdown/moving averages need complete split-adjusted closes, not dividend-return history back to the ATH; use available total returns for their actual comparison windows when they exist. Never switch return bases to rescue a failing result.

Use full public operating-company history for ATHs. For a SPAC, exclude the pre-business-combination shell history and explain the starting date. For operating history shorter than three years, report available ATH/return/trend data and mark the three-year test unavailable by age; that alone does not block entry. Existing route gates and small H caps still apply. For a mature listing, missing required history is **PH-unverified**, zero new capital until verified; never infer a lifetime ATH from a short chart window.

## Status and capital

- **PH-warning:** no new closing ATH for ≥3 years. Show the date/age prominently and explain whether the business has delivered per-share progress over that period. This warning alone does not block a qualified name.
- **PH-impaired:** all three hold: closing ATH ≥3 years old, drawdown from it ≥30%, and three-year underperformance versus the selected benchmark ≥20 percentage points on the disclosed total-return or permitted price-return basis. Unless recovery below is verified, zero new capital in A–E/F/G/H/M/D, regardless of a headline fundamental pass. Keep the underlying route verdict, append PH-impaired, and move the name to a turnaround watchlist instead of the investable ranking. A theme or cheap multiple cannot clear the overlay.
- **PH-recovery:** an impaired name has **≥10 consecutive completed trading closes** above its contemporaneous 200DMA; **either** today's 200DMA is higher than 20 completed sessions earlier **or** the 50DMA is above the 200DMA AND rising (higher than its previous completed-session value); **either** trailing three-month **or** six-month relative return is ≥0 percentage points on the disclosed consistent total-return or permitted price-return basis (one verified qualifying window suffices even if the other is negative or unavailable); **and** the latest print or H checkpoint verifies its full selected route. An intraday tenth close does not count. No new ATH is needed. Initial new allocation ≤5.0% of sleeve at cost AND ≤1.25% of portfolio, or the smaller selected-route initial cap (H, M and D: ≤2.5%/0.60%; G: ≤5.0%/1.25%). Full route sizing can resume only after the following clean print/checkpoint while recovery remains intact. This is staging for a fully qualified name, not a waiver of FT, A, G, H, M or D tests. F retains its separate limits, F2 timing and F5 accounting, alongside applicable PH staging.

For a held PH-impaired name, freeze additions and run a full business/thesis re-test. Price impairment alone is not an automatic sale; selected-route hard failures and written #15/#19 exits still govern. If recovery fails before normal sizing resumes, additions freeze again. For already-held names entering recovery, cap total exposure at the recovery ceiling for new additions; if the existing position exceeds it, no additions until the subsequent confirmation. Do not force a sale merely to reach the recovery entry cap.

For A–E/G/H/M/D, **new-entry PH-recovery** allocations join the combined #14a/G/H/M/D budget (≤35% of sleeve at cost AND ≤8% of portfolio); count a position once. Existing verified **A–E Core holdings** entering PH-recovery, including their historical cost basis, do **not** consume this non-core budget. Their additions remain frozen/staged as above, and all their cost continues to count toward ordinary #16 name/factor limits. The exception does not turn an unverified/conditional holding, existing G/H/M/D or a starter into Core. A normal fully qualified new-entry recovery position leaves recovery-budget accounting only after the following confirmation. Existing G/H/M/D/starter recovery positions remain counted at their full cost once, subject to the same route limits and smaller initial stage where applicable; do not count only a hypothetical new tranche to invent headroom. If the counted group is over budget, freeze its additions without a forced sale solely from relabeling. F stays outside the combined budget. Preserve cost basis and tranche history.

Write the ATH date/age, drawdown, benchmark return gap, trend checks, status and numeric recovery requirements in the roster at a fresh screen. Historical ALGN or other status labels cannot establish PH eligibility; verify the complete price history and current measurements. Thresholds reflect the user's preference and are not a backtested timing signal or a guarantee of recovery.
