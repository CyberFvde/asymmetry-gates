# PH — Price health and stale highs (Oct 2, 2026, revision 3)

Read for every equity screen. The user wants prolonged failure to recover an old high to affect eligibility and ranking. Fundamental quality and market performance are separate assessments; both must be visible. A fresh ATH is not required, and a recent rally alone does not qualify a business.

## Measurements

Use the last completed regular-session close and record the data date. Report the split-adjusted **closing ATH**, its date and age, drawdown from that ATH, three-year total return and its gap versus a preselected broad benchmark, current close versus the 200-day moving average, and today's 200DMA versus its value 20 trading sessions ago.

Use consistent price/total-return series, dates and currency. ATH/drawdown/200DMA use split-adjusted closes; relative returns include reinvested dividends and relevant distribution adjustments. Default benchmark: S&P 500 total return for US listings, an appropriate broad local-market total-return benchmark for non-US listings, fixed before seeing the result. State it; do not choose a weaker benchmark to obtain a pass. The return gap is stock total return minus benchmark total return in **percentage points**, not a ratio of percentages. Record both intraday and closing highs only if clearly distinguished; the test uses closing highs.

Use full public operating-company history for ATHs. For a SPAC, exclude the pre-business-combination shell history and explain the starting date. For operating history shorter than three years, report available ATH/return/trend data and mark the three-year test unavailable by age; that alone does not block entry. Existing route gates and small H caps still apply. For a mature listing, missing required history is **PH-unverified**, zero new capital until verified; never infer a lifetime ATH from a short chart window.

## Status and capital

- **PH-warning:** no new closing ATH for ≥3 years. Show the date/age prominently and explain whether the business has delivered per-share progress over that period. This warning alone does not block a qualified name.
- **PH-impaired:** all three hold: closing ATH ≥3 years old, drawdown from it ≥30%, and three-year total-return underperformance versus the selected benchmark ≥20 percentage points. Unless recovery below is verified, zero new capital in A–E/F/G/H, regardless of a headline fundamental pass. Keep the underlying route verdict, append PH-impaired, and move the name to a turnaround watchlist instead of the investable ranking. A theme or cheap multiple cannot clear the overlay.
- **PH-recovery:** an impaired name has ≥20 consecutive completed trading closes above its contemporaneous 200DMA; today's 200DMA is higher than 20 sessions earlier; trailing six-month relative total return is ≥0 percentage points; and the latest print or H checkpoint verifies its full selected route. No new ATH is needed. Initial new allocation ≤2.5% of sleeve at cost AND ≤0.5% of portfolio, or the smaller selected-route initial cap. Full route sizing can resume only after the following clean print/checkpoint while recovery remains intact. This is a staging limit on a fully qualified name, not a waiver of FT, A, G or H tests. F retains its separate limits, F2 timing and F5 accounting.

For a held PH-impaired name, freeze additions and run a full business/thesis re-test. Price impairment alone is not an automatic sale; selected-route hard failures and written #15/#17/#19 exits still govern. If recovery fails before normal sizing resumes, additions freeze again. For already-held names entering recovery, cap total exposure at the recovery ceiling for new additions; if the existing position exceeds it, no additions until the subsequent confirmation. Do not force a sale merely to reach the recovery entry cap.

For A–E/G/H, PH-recovery allocations join the existing combined #14a/G/H budget (≤20% of sleeve at cost AND ≤4% of portfolio); count a position once. A normal fully qualified position leaves the recovery portion of that budget only after the following confirmation. If reclassifying an existing holding puts the combined group over budget, count its full cost basis and phase in the cap through freezes on additions across that group, without a forced sale solely from relabeling. The freeze lasts until confirmation removes a qualified core holding from recovery accounting or group cost otherwise falls within the cap; do not invent headroom by counting only a hypothetical new tranche. F stays outside that combined budget. Preserve cost basis and tranche history.

Write the ATH date/age, drawdown, benchmark return gap, trend checks, status and numeric recovery requirements in the roster at a fresh screen. Historical ALGN or other status labels cannot establish PH eligibility; verify the complete price history and current measurements. Thresholds reflect the user's preference and are not a backtested timing signal or a guarantee of recovery.
