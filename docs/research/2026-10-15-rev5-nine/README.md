## Valuation and funding interpretation

**ALGM:** the reproducible annual cash/share model retains cash once, pays financial debt, excludes restricted cash, includes known unvested RSUs/max PSUs and additional ordinary dilution, and uses restrained multiples against its completed-FY median and a declared profitable analog peer set. Bear/base/bull terminal values are approximately **$16.19 / $55.63 / $105.28**. The base-return ceiling is ~$31.57; the 60%-bear-floor ceiling is **$26.98**, which binds. At the current entry the base is ~11.54%/year and bear ~50.25% of entry. These results are conditional on explicit estimates, not an approved limit order or proof that intrinsic value cannot be higher. A fully sourced award/claim register and remaining G checks still matter below that price. Peer ratios were observed Oct 15 and are disclosed separately from the Oct 14 stock entry.

**PL:** its annual H sensitivity models cash PP&E, software, employee withholding, notes, potential conversion and dilution. It compares cash maturity with full physical conversion without taking both principal and the same conversion shares as simultaneous claims; no capped-call offset is assumed. A plausible cash-quality destination alone does not ensure an attractive per-share return. Bear/base/bull terminal values are approximately **$0.73 / $12.51 / $37.71**, or **−44.8% / −2.4% / +21.7%** annually at $14.13. The base return-only ceiling is approximately **$5.03**, before the unresolved funding/insider/entry checks. This is a scenario sensitivity using profitable-peer context, not a fully verified intrinsic-value ceiling. Failed commercialization has zero equity value: 100% of the position can be lost.

**AVAV/KRMN:** explicit annual cash/share sensitivities now supplement the published operating anchors. Base terminal values of ~$181.34 / $61.11 imply ~8.5% / 15.2% annual returns, below H's 20% hurdle at the quoted entries. Their conditional return-only price outputs (~$72.88 / $24.56) use assumed terminal multiples whose profitable-peer validation is incomplete; they are not verified entry ceilings. Illustrative H2 stresses include lower revenue and cash uses rather than treating backlog or EBITDA as funding. KRMN's completed Walker acquisition is paired with its completed $100M loan draw; the entire purchase is not deducted from June cash while ignoring that financing. The reports distinguish published operating anchors, our sensitivities and genuinely unverified facts. No unsupported five-year EBITDA multiple is promoted into a proven H4 rejection.

**ASTS:** July's $1.15B convertible issuance supplies about **$1.0198B usable cash after issuance costs and capped calls**, before subsequent burn. $520M of Ligado's $550M consideration was already paid by June; only the remaining $30M belongs in the forward obligation bridge. The issuer's ability to slow construction matters: a flat high-buildout burn stress is a sensitivity, not proof that every funded plan fails.
