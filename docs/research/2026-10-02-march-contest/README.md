### What the leverage buys, and costs

March 31 occurs **16 days before April expiration**, so expiry intrinsic payoffs are not contest returns. Calls are marked with a European Black–Scholes approximation to non-dividend American calls, a stated 3.5% rate, and March IV at **75% of each observed IV**. These are theoretical values, not future bids. Fees, spreads, financing frictions, changing rates and any early exercise are excluded. A 10% haircut to every theoretical mark was rejected because it would mark deep-in-the-money calls below intrinsic value in bull cases. Future executable liquidation values remain unknown.
