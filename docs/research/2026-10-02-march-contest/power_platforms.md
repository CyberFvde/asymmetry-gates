## Illustrative five-year diluted cash models and entry ceilings

These are transparent own models for G research, **not verified G5 passes**. Annual horizon FY27–FY31; cash FCF=company OCF minus cash PP&E, after ordinary operating interest/taxes. No disposal proceeds or SBC addback; margins are modeled normalized cash margins.
