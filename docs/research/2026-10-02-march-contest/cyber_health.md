## PODD five-year G5 draft — assumptions, not a capital pass

The machine-readable annual model is `PODD_g5_draft.json`. Starting revenue is 3.62975B NTM; starting fully diluted shares are 70.578M (69.403M diluted WASO plus 1.175M presently anti-dilutive awards), with 0.5% annual ordinary dilution and no assumed buybacks. Cash starts at 534.9M. Cash PP&E is 5% of revenue throughout, close to FY2027's 186.8M /3.743B. Cash conversion is modeled after cash interest and all PP&E, with no adjusted-FCF addbacks. It pays 510.7M of term/equipment/plant principal by maturity, retains a further 97M guarantee stress and leaves the 450M 2033 note outstanding. The remaining note's interest stays in modeled OCF.

One valuation convention is used: terminal **ex-cash equity P/FCF** times levered company FCF, plus retained cash, divided by fully diluted shares. Debt is embodied in cash interest, the repayment schedule and the capital structure attached to the equity multiple; it is not subtracted again as if this were an enterprise-value model. No dividends are assumed and retained cash is added only once. Cash interest income from accumulated cash and debt-paydown interest savings are omitted, conservatively. New manufacturing needs stay inside the 5%-revenue capex assumption; a buildout beyond it would lower the values.
